# Human-facing work presentation

The primary views answer three questions: what is progressing, what is waiting, and what needs the human. Routine agent coordination belongs in expandable detail.

- **Streams:** one short update per card, with meaningful attention/activity/waiting signals. Delivery counts describe available outputs, not a completion percentage. Opening a stream immediately shows its work and feeding streams in a selectable Map/List, with real prerequisite links and human requests. The selected item provides purpose, progress, blockers and direct unlocks; delivered work stays visible and quieter. Scope, management and history are secondary. Empty sections, duplicate status and routine progress boilerplate are omitted.
- **Changes:** compact title/status/stream rows. Detail keeps purpose, latest update, unmet prerequisites and result links readable. Routine completion controls, success criteria, full reports, dependency assessments, claims, resource names, base refs and history remain available on demand.
- **Needs you:** the request first, its immediate unlock next. A short request, stream context and optional Open chat link keep the card simple; full conditions, impact and response provenance remain in work details/history. Proposal, coverage, delivery and review statuses do not create human requests.

Long prose gets a short plain-text preview with explicit expansion to the full safe Markdown. Nothing is removed from stored work or MCP context. Detailed evidence retains attribution, timestamps and revisions; a concise Delivered label does not mean owner-completed or independently verified. Human-facing stream summaries should be short and outcome-focused. Include technical detail when directly related to the ask or needed for behavior, tradeoffs, risks or decisions; keep routine operational detail in findings/evidence.

The card depends on useful agent-written content: name a concrete goal with a verb in the user's terms and describe progress toward it. Version 0.4.1 / workflow 1.3.1 supplies consistent [writing guidance and examples](agent-workflow.md#writing-work-for-the-human) through MCP, generated client skills, project instructions, tool help and lifecycle reminders. It adds no card fields and does not automatically rename or broaden existing streams.

As of 0.5.0, accept/decline and coverage-management controls are removed. Needs you cards have no approval or response form; collaboration happens in the agent chat. Ordinary pause/finish/completion controls remain under management disclosures. Existing decisions and unresolved question/action records are preserved. This does not implement immutable plans or the rest of the earlier parked redesign.

Shared typography, color, spacing, focus and reduced-motion rules continue to follow [visual-design.md](visual-design.md). Browser checks exercise both the quiet default and deliberate expansion for historical/manual workflows, including human responses, dependent navigation, Markdown/link safety, session recovery and narrow layouts.

## Stream work map (0.6.0)

Map groups show stream membership; arrows show only recorded prerequisites, never inferred sequence or independence. Solid arrows are start gates; dashed arrows are delivery gates. Selection highlights direct relationships and provides their reasons and attributed satisfaction. Unknown/stale assessments remain explicit. Questions and human actions share the view, with optional safe chat links; answered/done requests remain quiet context. Full request text is retrieved on selection. List mode keeps the same selection and relationships and is automatic in narrow views.

The read-only projection is bounded to 40 stream groups, 160 core nodes, 320 edges and 40 linked reference nodes. Truncation is disclosed; focus a feeding stream or browse changes to continue. Context/history have their own pagination and cannot page work away. Stage-valid loops are retained rather than rejected or presented as an execution order. No worker, scheduling, approval workflow or stored layout is added.
